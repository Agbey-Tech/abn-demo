Why the Network exists

Ministry has changed.
The infrastructure hasn’t kept up.

The demands on senior pastors have grown: organisational complexity, public scrutiny, the pressure of digital ministry, and the emotional weight of shepherding congregations through cultural turbulence.

Most peer structures built for ministry leaders were designed for a different era: conferences with thousands of attendees, denominations that prioritise alignment over honesty, coaching programs that treat every leader the same.

The ABN Network is being built as the other kind of room, where the people across the table carry the same weight and aren’t trying to sell you something, impress you or protect their position.

Community values

The structure
behind the trust.

01

No celebrities. No headliners.

No featured speakers at an ABN gathering. No one is brought in to impress the room.

02

Confidentiality is structural.

Nothing posted from inside the room, no one quoted without consent, and no marketing made from what members share.

03

Intentionally small. Always.

Gatherings are sized for real conversation, not to maximise reach. Small is the feature, not the constraint.

04

Built to compound, not to consume.

The value builds over time, with each gathering, each peer relationship and each honest conversation.

Not a fit right now

The Network is not designed for emerging leaders, early-career associate pastors, or anyone looking for a structured curriculum. The room’s value depends on everyone in it leading at a similar level.

Membership FAQs

Common questions.

Who is the Network designed for?+

Senior pastors, founding pastors, executive pastors and ministry leaders carrying significant organisational responsibility. If you're unsure whether you're a fit, the consideration call is the right next step.

How does the invitation process work?+

Submit a brief consideration request. We review every submission personally, respond within 48 hours and follow up with a private 20-minute call. After the call you receive a clear answer.

Where do gatherings take place?+

ABN is a nationwide network. Gatherings are planned in major ministry cities across the US. Location details are shared after membership is confirmed.

What does membership include?+

The Shepherd's Roundtable (quarterly), curated dinners and retreats, and connection between gatherings.

Is ABN connected to a denomination?+

No. ABN is independent and non-denominational, and designed to serve leaders across the spectrum of faith traditions.
