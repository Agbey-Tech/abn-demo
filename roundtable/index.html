The Network’s signature experience

The Shepherd’s
Roundtable.

A different room. A deeper conversation. A private, invitation-only gathering for senior pastors and ministry leaders, being developed for honest dialogue that doesn’t happen in public.

Why the Roundtable exists

No speakers. No sponsors.
No audience.

Just leaders who have earned their seats, and the discipline to leave titles at the door.

Rules of the room

The structure that earns the trust.

01

No speakers. No experts.

Everyone in the room is a peer. No one presents, and every voice carries equal weight.

02

No public agenda.

What’s discussed stays in the room: no social media, no attributed quotes, no marketing made from what is shared.

03

Curated, not open.

Every seat is considered. The value depends on the calibre and compatibility of who sits at the table.

04

Titles stay at the door.

Inside, you are a leader in a room of leaders: a peer doing the same hard work.

The format

Four deliberate phases.

01

Opening context

A brief framing: a question or theme surfaced from the group ahead of time, to anchor the conversation without controlling it.

02

Open-table conversation

The core of the Roundtable. Leaders talk to each other, not to a facilitator.

03

Closing commitments

Each leader names one decision, question or shift they’re taking with them.

04

Follow-through

Connection continues between gatherings.

The inaugural gathering

June 2027.
The first room.

The first Shepherd’s Roundtable is planned for June 2027 with the founding cohort of the ABN Network.

Quarterly sessions, four times a year, with 8–12 leaders. Founding invitations are planned to begin in February 2027. Location is shared with invited leaders. Participation is subject to review and formal invitation.
